The Principle of Proportional Duty (PPD)

A mathematical framework for moral responsibility under uncertainty that formalizes the relationship between epistemic humility, knowledge quality, and moral duty.

The PPD framework demonstrates that responsible action scales with both what we know and our awareness of what we don't know—a principle with applications spanning AI governance, medical ethics, and high-stakes decision-making.
